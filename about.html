OUR HERITAGE

Dedicated to Ceylon’s Finest Natural Gemstones

Welcome to Shaheer Gems, where the natural beauty of precious gemstones meets elegance and craftsmanship.

Based in Sri Lanka, the historically celebrated "Ratna Dweepa" (Island of Gems), Shaheer Gems is dedicated to presenting an authentic selection of natural gemstones to collectors, connoisseurs, and fine jewelry designers who appreciate rarity, brilliance, and enduring value.

Our roots lie in ethical sourcing directly from primary Sri Lankan alluvial deposits and historic gem fields including Ratnapura, Elahera, and Balangoda. Each rough crystal is carefully evaluated and cut by master lapidaries to maximize light performance, optical symmetry, and color purity.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To source, curate, and supply 100% natural and untreated precious gemstones with absolute disclosure, providing global and local clients with high-caliber gemstones, transparent certifications, and unmatched personal consultation.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a distinguished symbol of Ceylon gemstone excellence, upholding Sri Lanka’s revered lapidary heritage while providing modern, accessible digital cataloging and bespoke acquisition services.
